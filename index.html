About

I'm an MBA candidate at INSEAD (Class of July 2027) and a former Senior Associate Consultant at Bain & Company in Saudi Arabia, where I worked on strategy engagements across the Middle East.

Egyptian by background, I grew up in a German school, studied finance at an American university, and have since lived and worked in Dubai, Riyadh, France and Singapore. Alongside the MBA I build financial models for founders and work on early-stage ventures.
